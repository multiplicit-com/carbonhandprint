description: A dated reading list of articles, research and reports on the carbon handprint concept since 2007.
---

<ol class="timeline">
  <li><span class="yr">2007</span><span><a href="https://www.ceeindia.org/handprint.php">Handprint</a>, Centre for Environment Education (India). The education programme presented at the Fourth International Conference on Environmental Education.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2013</span><span><a href="https://kkh.ltrr.arizona.edu/kkh/natsgc/pdfs-2014/Our-Environmental-Handprint.pdf">Our Environmental Handprint: The Good We Do</a>, Jon Biemer, Willow Dixon and Natalia Blackburn, IEEE Conference on Technologies for Sustainability. Case studies in wind power, solar cookers and green building.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Nov 2015</span><span><a href="https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.mit.edu/dist/0/1158/files/2025/01/Norris-2015-Handprint-Based-NetPositive-Assessment_2.pdf">Handprint-Based NetPositive Assessment</a>, Gregory Norris, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Sets out how to count handprints against business as usual, so an organisation can aim for a handprint bigger than its footprint.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2018</span><span><a href="https://sarjaweb.vtt.fi/julkaisut/muut/2018/Carbon_Handprint_Guide.pdf">Carbon Handprint Guide</a>, VTT and LUT University. The first formal method for companies.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Dec 2018</span><span><a href="https://phys.org/news/2018-12-environmental-indicator-positive-climate-impacts.html">New environmental indicator for positive climate impacts</a>, VTT via Phys.org. The launch announcement, naming Nokia, Neste, KONE and other partners.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2019</span><span><a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959652618329664">Carbon handprint: an approach to assess the positive climate impacts of products demonstrated via renewable diesel case</a>, Kaisa Grönman and colleagues, <em>Journal of Cleaner Production</em>. The peer-reviewed paper behind the VTT/LUT method.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Feb 2021</span><span><a href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11367-021-01874-5">Sustainability Health Initiative for NetPositive Enterprise handprint methodological framework</a>, Gregory Norris and colleagues at MIT, <em>International Journal of Life Cycle Assessment</em>.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">May 2021</span><span><a href="https://www.vttresearch.com/en/news-and-ideas/carbon-handprint-evolved-environmental-handprint-vtt-and-lut-developed-positive">Carbon handprint evolved into an environmental handprint</a>, VTT. The 2.0 guide, covering water, air, nutrients and resources.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Oct 2021</span><span><a href="https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/20/11286">Exploring Social Sustainability Handprint, Part 1</a>, Roope Husgafvel, <em>Sustainability</em>. Takes the handprint beyond the environment into social impact.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Aug 2023</span><span><a href="https://ukgbc.org/news/whats-a-carbon-handprint-understanding-your-organisations-positive-impact/">What's a carbon handprint? Understanding your organisation's positive impact</a>, Maxim Goreansky and Lotta Marjamäki of Helvar, UK Green Building Council. A member article using the VTT definition, with a lighting controls example.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Oct 2023</span><span><a href="https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09613218.2023.2266020">Carbon handprint: a review of potential climate benefits of buildings</a>, Leonora Charlotte Malabi Eberhardt, Matti Kuittinen, Tarja Häkkinen, Caroline Moinel, Sylviane Nibel and Harpa Birgisdóttir (Aalborg University, Aalto University, SYKE and CSTB), <em>Building Research &amp; Information</em>.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2016 to 2024</span><span><a href="https://handprint.fi/">Handprint</a>, VTT and LUT University. The home of the Finnish handprint research projects: Carbon Handprint, Environmental Handprint and Carbon Neutrality Empowered by Handprint.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">May 2024</span><span><a href="https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/climatechange/symposia/202405/Documents/Anthony%20Darcy.pdf">Nokia's ESG strategy: maximise the handprint, minimise the footprint</a>, Anthony D'Arcy, ITU climate change symposium.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Nov 2025</span><span><a href="https://www.mediekultur.dk/article/view/152898">The dual climate impact of news media</a>, Meri Frig, Maarit Jaakkola and Laura Olkkonen, <em>MedieKultur</em>. How Nordic newsrooms think about their footprint and their handprint.</span></li>
</ol>
