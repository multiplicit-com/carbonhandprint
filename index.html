Most of us have heard of a carbon footprint. Your carbon handprint is the other side of it: the good you do.

Cycling to work, talking a neighbour into solar panels, getting your school to recycle properly. It all counts, and no contribution is too small.

More about the idea What can I do?

Why a handprint?

For years, climate action has been framed around carbon footprints.

The carbon handprint is meant as something more positive, that might bring new people into the environmental movement. A tool to help describe and measure positive contributions for a new generation of environmentalists.

The handprint isn't meant to replace the footprint or excuse it. It's a way of noticing the good things people do, and encouraging more of them.

Since then the idea has been picked up by schools, researchers and some very large companies, including Nokia. The history page has more on that, and our reading list collects the best articles and research on the idea.

Nobody owns the term Carbon Handprint, so please feel free to use it.
