Your footprint is the harm. Your handprint is the help.

A carbon footprint measures the emissions we cause. A carbon handprint recognises the positive difference we make: the emissions we help avoid, the people we bring along, the changes we start. No contribution is too small.

What is a carbon handprint? Grow yours

Two sides of the same hand

👣 Carbon footprint

The greenhouse gases released by what you, your household or your organisation does.

  • Measures harm
  • Goal: make it smaller
  • Ends at your own front door

✋ Carbon handprint

The emissions you help others avoid, and the positive action you inspire or enable beyond your own life.

  • Measures help
  • Goal: make it bigger
  • Reaches as far as your influence does

The handprint is not a replacement for the footprint, or a way to excuse it. It is a second lens that keeps the focus on what we can do, not only on what we should stop doing.

Where the idea is today

For people

Everyday action that spreads: cycling to work and getting colleagues to join, pushing for a school solar panel, voting and campaigning for climate policy.

For organisations

Finnish researchers at VTT and LUT University publish a method for companies to calculate the emissions their products help customers avoid. Nokia, one of its first partners, now frames its whole sustainability strategy around handprint and footprint.

For communities

Educators and campaigners use "handprint" to turn climate worry into collective, structural action.
