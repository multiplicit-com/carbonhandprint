Good ideas tend to appear in several places at once, and the handprint is one of them. The image of a positive hand beside a negative foot is a natural one, and it is quite possible that several people arrived at the name independently, for slightly different purposes. This is our best understanding of how it unfolded. There is also a [reading list](/reading/) of articles and research. If you know of an earlier or missing use, [please tell us](/contact/).

## Our position

Nobody owns the term Carbon Handprint, and nobody should. We put it out freely in 2007 and we release it freely now. Anyone may use it: individuals, schools, campaigners, researchers and businesses.

Our goal has not changed. It is to reinforce the environmental message by reminding everyone that they can make a difference.

We ask one thing of anyone using the term: be honest. A handprint sits alongside a footprint. It must never be used to hide one.
