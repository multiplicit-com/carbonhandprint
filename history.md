Good ideas tend to appear in several places at once, and the handprint is one of them. Several groups arrived at the same image, the positive mark of a hand beside the negative mark of a foot, independently and for slightly different purposes. This is our best understanding of how it unfolded. If you know of an earlier or missing use, [please tell us](/about/#get-in-touch).

<ol class="timeline">
  <li><span class="yr">2007</span> · The <a href="https://www.ceeindia.org/handprint.php">Centre for Environment Education</a> (CEE) in India launches <strong>"Handprint"</strong> as a symbol of positive action for sustainability at the Fourth International Conference on Environmental Education in Ahmedabad. It goes on to be used widely in education for sustainable development.</li>
  <li><span class="yr">Late 2000s</span> · <strong>carbonhandprint.org</strong> launches as a website where anyone could record their pledges and achievements for the environment, with the aim of letting a personal pledge and a data centre's energy figures sit side by side in one open record. As far as we know it was among the first places to publish the specific phrase "carbon handprint" online, though it was almost certainly used informally before then.</li>
  <!-- TODO (Steve): add the launch year and any early history you can substantiate, e.g. first registration date or early press. -->
  <li><span class="yr">Early 2010s</span> · Gregory Norris at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and its <a href="https://hwpi.harvard.edu/files/chge/files/SHINE-Handprints_0_0.pdf">SHINE initiative</a> popularise <strong>"handprinting"</strong>: measuring the footprint reductions a person causes in others, so that a handprint can grow bigger than one's own footprint.</li>
  <li><span class="yr">2018</span> · <a href="https://www.vttresearch.com/">VTT</a> and <a href="https://www.lut.fi/">LUT University</a> in Finland publish the <a href="https://sarjaweb.vtt.fi/julkaisut/muut/2018/Carbon_Handprint_Guide.pdf">Carbon Handprint Guide</a>, a method for companies to quantify the emissions their products help customers avoid. It was developed from 2016 to 2018 with Finnish companies including <strong>Nokia</strong>, Neste and KONE (<a href="https://phys.org/news/2018-12-environmental-indicator-positive-climate-impacts.html">announcement</a>).</li>
  <li><span class="yr">2021</span> · After a two-year project with 16 companies, VTT and LUT release <a href="https://sarjaweb.vtt.fi/julkaisut/muut/2021/Carbon_handprint_guide_2021.pdf">guide version 2.0</a> and widen the carbon handprint into an <a href="https://www.vttresearch.com/en/news-and-ideas/carbon-handprint-evolved-environmental-handprint-vtt-and-lut-developed-positive">environmental handprint</a> covering air, water, nutrients and resources.</li>
  <li><span class="yr">2020s</span> · <strong>Nokia</strong> builds the idea into its company strategy, describing its ESG goals as working to <a href="https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/climatechange/symposia/202405/Documents/Anthony%20Darcy.pdf">"maximize our handprint and minimize our footprint"</a>, and calling the benefits of connectivity <a href="https://www.nokia.com/about-us/sustainability/all-about-zero/">"the handprint of digitalization"</a>.</li>
  <li><span class="yr">2020s</span> · Other businesses such as <a href="https://www.neste.com/news-and-insights/sustainability/carbon-handprint-and-footprint-explained">Neste</a> report handprints next to footprints. Campaigners such as <a href="https://www.leave-it-in-the-ground.org/projects/carbon-footprints-to-handprints/">LINGO</a> use "footprints to handprints" to argue for structural, collective action. Writers like <a href="https://earthbound.report/2026/01/09/what-is-a-carbon-handprint/">The Earthbound Report</a> introduce the term to new audiences.</li>
  <li><span class="yr">2026</span> · carbonhandprint.org is rebuilt, with an updated guide and a blog.</li>
</ol>

## Our view

Nobody owns the term Carbon Handprint, and we don't think anyone should. This site's goal is the same as it always was: to reinforce the environmental message by reminding everyone that they can make a difference.

We welcome every serious use of the idea, from a schoolchild's pledge to a company's verified product assessment. What matters is that handprints are honest: added to an honest footprint, never used to hide one.
