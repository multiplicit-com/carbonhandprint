Good ideas tend to appear in several places at once, and the handprint is one of them. The image of a positive hand beside a negative foot is a natural one, and it is quite possible that several people arrived at the name independently, for slightly different purposes. This is our best understanding of how it unfolded. If you know of an earlier or missing use, [please tell us](/about/#get-in-touch).

<ol class="timeline">
  <li><span class="yr">Summer 2007</span><span><strong>carbonhandprint.org</strong> launches as a website where anyone could record their pledges and achievements for the environment, with the aim of letting a personal pledge and a data centre's energy figures sit side by side in one open record. To our knowledge it was among the first places to publish the phrase "carbon handprint" online.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">November 2007</span><span>The <a href="https://www.ceeindia.org/handprint.php">Centre for Environment Education</a> (CEE) in India presents <strong>"Handprint"</strong> as a symbol of positive action for sustainability at the Fourth International Conference on Environmental Education in Ahmedabad. It goes on to be used widely in education for sustainable development.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">Early 2010s</span><span>Gregory Norris at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and its <a href="https://hwpi.harvard.edu/files/chge/files/SHINE-Handprints_0_0.pdf">SHINE initiative</a> popularise <strong>"handprinting"</strong>: measuring the footprint reductions a person causes in others, so that a handprint can grow bigger than one's own footprint.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2018</span><span><a href="https://www.vttresearch.com/">VTT</a> and <a href="https://www.lut.fi/">LUT University</a> in Finland publish the <a href="https://sarjaweb.vtt.fi/julkaisut/muut/2018/Carbon_Handprint_Guide.pdf">Carbon Handprint Guide</a>, a method for companies to quantify the emissions their products help customers avoid. It was developed from 2016 to 2018 with Finnish companies including <strong>Nokia</strong>, Neste and KONE (<a href="https://phys.org/news/2018-12-environmental-indicator-positive-climate-impacts.html">announcement</a>).</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2021</span><span>After a two-year project with 16 companies, VTT and LUT release <a href="https://sarjaweb.vtt.fi/julkaisut/muut/2021/Carbon_handprint_guide_2021.pdf">guide version 2.0</a> and widen the carbon handprint into an <a href="https://www.vttresearch.com/en/news-and-ideas/carbon-handprint-evolved-environmental-handprint-vtt-and-lut-developed-positive">environmental handprint</a> covering air, water, nutrients and resources.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2020s</span><span><strong>Nokia</strong> builds the idea into its company strategy, describing its ESG goals as working to <a href="https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/climatechange/symposia/202405/Documents/Anthony%20Darcy.pdf">"maximize our handprint and minimize our footprint"</a>, and calling the benefits of connectivity <a href="https://www.nokia.com/about-us/sustainability/all-about-zero/">"the handprint of digitalization"</a>.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2020s</span><span>Other businesses such as <a href="https://www.neste.com/news-and-insights/sustainability/carbon-handprint-and-footprint-explained">Neste</a> report handprints next to footprints. Campaigners such as <a href="https://www.leave-it-in-the-ground.org/projects/carbon-footprints-to-handprints/">LINGO</a> use "footprints to handprints" to argue for structural, collective action. Writers like <a href="https://earthbound.report/2026/01/09/what-is-a-carbon-handprint/">The Earthbound Report</a> introduce the term to new audiences.</span></li>
  <li><span class="yr">2026</span><span>carbonhandprint.org is rebuilt, with an updated guide and a blog.</span></li>
</ol>

## Our position

Nobody owns the term Carbon Handprint, and nobody should. We put it out freely in 2007 and we release it freely now. Anyone may use it: individuals, schools, campaigners, researchers and businesses.

Our goal has not changed. It is to reinforce the environmental message by reminding everyone that they can make a difference.

We ask one thing of anyone using the term: be honest. A handprint sits alongside a footprint. It must never be used to hide one.
