This page explains what happens to information about you when you visit carbonhandprint.org. The short version: we don't use cookies, we don't run analytics or advertising, and we don't track you.

## What we collect

**Nothing directly.** The site has no accounts, forms, comments, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

**Server logs.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. We can't see these logs. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for how GitHub handles them.

**Images from other sites.** Some pages show pictures that load from elsewhere: preview images on news items load from the publisher's own website, and the xkcd comic on the Paris Agreement page loads from xkcd.com. When your browser fetches those images, that site receives your IP address, as with any web request. We ask browsers not to send which page you were on when they do this.

**Links.** Links to other websites, including Facebook, take you to sites with their own privacy policies. Nothing from Facebook or other social networks is embedded on our pages.

## If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply and to deal with what you wrote about, such as a correction. We don't add you to any mailing list or pass your details to anyone else, and we delete messages we no longer need.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. In practice the only information we could hold is an email you've sent us. [Get in touch](/contact/) and we'll help. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Changes

If anything here changes, for example if we ever add analytics, we'll update this page first.
