carbonhandprint.org is an independent, non-commercial site about the carbon handprint and climate action. This page sets out how we work.

## What we publish

- **Articles**, written by us, explaining ideas, events and research.
- **News links** to reporting and research elsewhere, with a short note from us.
- **In brief** items: short, sourced facts.

Articles show the author, the date first published and, if changed significantly, the date updated.

## Sources

We link to the original source wherever we can, and every fact in an in-brief item or country summary links to where it came from.

We favour authoritative sources: governments and parliaments, UN bodies, standards organisations, universities and peer-reviewed journals, recognised research institutes, large companies reporting on their own work, and established news organisations. We check a source before linking to it. We have a responsibility to disseminate verifiable information, so we may not be able to feature smaller blogs or new sites whose authority we can't establish, even when it is an interesting topic.

When a claim rests on one organisation's own account, we say so. For example, "the company says" rather than stating it as fact.

## Independence

We don't take payment for coverage, and we don't run sponsored content or affiliate links. Nobody owns the term Carbon Handprint, including us.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we correct it as soon as we can. Corrections that change the meaning of an article are noted at the end of it, with the date. Small fixes such as typos and broken links are made without a note.
