The award, run by Finland's Climate Leadership Coalition and the Vancouver Economic Commission, was presented at Climate Week NYC. Schneider Electric won for its Altivar variable speed drives and SSAB for HYBRIT fossil-free steel, with honorary certificates for Neste, Schneider's RM AirSeT switchgear and Polar Night Energy's sand battery. Entries were judged using the VTT/LUT carbon handprint method. More at [carbonhandprintaward.com](https://carbonhandprintaward.com/).
