<p>Happy New Year (nearly!). As 2018 draws to a close we should look back on one of our most important achievements and reflect on what happened since then.</p>
<p>2016 saw the formalization of the Paris Agreement - a landmark accord to limit the impact of humans and industrialization on our planet. It is not a cure-all, but it is the first time so many nations have agreed to work in the common good.</p>
<p>In brief the Paris agreement was a commitment to limit global temperature increases to 2 degrees above pre-industrial levels while  strengthening our ability to deal with the impact of global climate change. It happened several years ago, but it remains one of the most important environmental goals.</p>
<p>Since then progress has been mixed. Many countries moved to ratify the Paris agreement to bring it closer to reality, and the United States withdrew from it entirely to become the only country on Earth not to participate.</p>
<p>We don’t know what the changing political climate will bring but as it stands Paris is still a go! It is in our power to keep it that way. We need to keep reminding the world that protecting the environment is important to us and that translates into votes and support.</p>
<p>Meeting these targets will mean real changes and sacrifices for most countries involved, but it is vitally important. This will require truly global cooperation that could even help our planet become a friendlier, more understanding place. The Paris agreement really could be the first step in a journey of environmental and social change.</p>
<p>2019 presents a great opportunity for us to let the world know what matters to us - see you there!</p>
