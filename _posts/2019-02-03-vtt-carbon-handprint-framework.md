<p>A Finnish consortium including Nokia has developed a framework for calculating Carbon Handprint contributions.</p>
<p>VTT, LUT University and a consortium of Finnish companies cooperated to develop a way to calculate and communicate the carbon handprint of products. </p>
<p>The new methodology rewards benefits such as improved energy efficiency, reduced materials and climate-friendly raw materials with higher Carbon handprint scores.</p>
