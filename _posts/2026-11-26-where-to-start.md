When we started in 2007, "carbon handprint" was a phrase almost nobody used. The idea was simple: if your carbon footprint is the damage you do, your handprint could be a record of the good you do. We wanted a positive message that would recruit a new generation of environmentalists, rather than only telling people what to give up.

A lot has happened since. The handprint now turns up in classrooms, in Finnish research methods used by large companies, in corporate sustainability reports and in campaigns for structural change. Here's where to start:

- [What is a carbon handprint?](/what-is-a-carbon-handprint/) explains the idea in plain English, and how people and companies use it.
- [Grow your handprint](/grow-your-handprint/) has practical ideas that go beyond your own front door.
- [How the idea has grown](/history/) is a short timeline of the concept.

The principle hasn't changed. **Nobody owns the term Carbon Handprint**, and anyone is free to use it.
