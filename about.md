carbonhandprint.org is an open, non-commercial home for the carbon handprint idea: the positive counterpart to the carbon footprint.

The original site was built more than a decade ago as a place where anyone could record their achievements or promises for the environment. The internet, and the climate conversation, have changed a lot since then. This rebuild brings the site up to date with how the handprint is understood and used today, and gives it a blog so it can keep up.

## Open by default

Nobody owns the term Carbon Handprint. Most content on this site, and any future research or resources, is released under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/), so that teachers, writers and campaigners can build on it quickly and easily. Businesses are welcome to use it too. Just get in touch, since commercial use is not covered by the default licence.

## Where we'd like to go

At the moment, personal carbon handprints are largely anecdotal. We'd love to see an open, robust and free way to record handprint contributions, developed by the community. One that can hold a simple pledge to cycle to work alongside measured data from an organisation.

## Get in touch {#get-in-touch}

We're always looking for inspiring handprint stories, corrections, and people who want to help.
